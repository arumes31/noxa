Windows System.Speech, followed by an original synthesized double beep.
This recording is separate from the CC0 sample libraries listed below.
The checked-in WAV is used directly; no Windows voice engine is shipped.

Author: Luckius

Archive SHA-256: aa1889414310c7cfdd8dc291c19d4e4fc21ec987e9238b0d961fb72449c263a4

Speech: complete fixed noXa English and German sentences rendered offline.
English: Piper en_US-ljspeech-high, based on public-domain LJ Speech.
https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/
German: D33, Piper de_DE-mls-medium, speaker ID 9 (MLS source speaker 9132).
Model published by rhasspy / Michael Hansen:
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/1162a9173d0ce503555aed757976b7a9912eae4c/de/de_DE/mls/medium/MODEL_CARD
Based on Multilingual LibriSpeech (MLS), 2020:
Vineel Pratap, Qiantong Xu, Anuroop Sriram, Gabriel Synnaeve and Ronan Collobert.
https://www.openslr.org/94/
Dataset and German noXa recordings: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode
Changes: synthesized noXa sentences, pronunciation overrides, context cropping,
silence trimming, endpoint fades and loudness normalization. No endorsement implied.
Voice source revision: 1162a9173d0ce503555aed757976b7a9912eae4c
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices
Piper tooling and model weights are not included in the application.
