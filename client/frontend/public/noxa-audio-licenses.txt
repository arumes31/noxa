Windows System.Speech, followed by an original synthesized double beep.
This recording is separate from the CC0 sample libraries listed below.
Localized English/German poke speech replaces this cue. No Windows voice engine is shipped.

Speech: complete fixed noXa English and German sentences rendered offline.
Both languages use the synthetic ST13 voice ("Eisbrecher"), chosen from the
ST01-ST20 auditions. ST13 is a weighted blend of the Supertonic 3 preset voice
styles (M5 x 1.25, M1 x -0.25); no recording of a real person and no imitation of
an identifiable character was used. The earlier EA60/AN06 recordings are retired.
Supertonic 3 model weights: Supertone Inc., OpenRAIL-M.
https://huggingface.co/Supertone/supertonic-3
Supertonic code: MIT License, Copyright (c) 2025 Supertone Inc.
https://github.com/supertone-inc/supertonic
INT8 conversion and offline runtime: sherpa-onnx v1.13.8, Apache-2.0,
Xiaomi Corporation and contributors.
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
Model archive: sherpa-onnx-supertonic-3-tts-int8-2026-05-11.tar.bz2
Model archive SHA-256: 82fa96f91c4ef8abaae3a14a3f4153facf88bed821d1f7331cec2700f432c427

Model/tool licenses identify authoring sources; they do not automatically
establish the license of generated recordings. The model, its voice styles and
the runtime are not included in the application. The original noXa English/German
fixed recordings are distributed under the project's MIT license below.
Changes: generated complete noXa sentences, pronunciation respellings for German
loanwords, resampling to 24 kHz, silence trimming, DC removal, endpoint fades and
loudness normalization.
