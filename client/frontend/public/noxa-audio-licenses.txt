Speech: complete fixed noXa English and German sentences rendered offline.
English: selected synthetic EA60 voice, "Epic adventure narrator", authored with
Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign and reproduced from its original WAV and complete transcript
using Qwen3-TTS Base. Historical Piper/LJ Speech English WAVs are retired.

German: selected synthetic AN06 voice, authored with Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign
and reproduced from its original WAV and complete transcript using Qwen3-TTS Base.
